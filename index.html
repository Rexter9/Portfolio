Reliable systems, with AI where it counts.

01 · Workflow engine · Full stack

Relay

An n8n-style engine where a workflow is a graph of AI calls, HTTP requests, conditions, delays and human approval steps. Runs go through a Redis queue to a separate worker and every step is saved, so a run resumes after a crash. AI inputs are scanned for prompt injection before reaching the model.
